Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you might want to know before booking. Still curious about something? Just ask.

Are you accepting new clients at this time?

Yes, I have some openings. Please note this is an online only (tele-therapy) practice. Sessions are conducted via secure video calls. Clients must be physically residing in the state of Florida or Arizona at the time of each session.

Do you take insurance?

Yes I do! You can find the list of insurance under the "Investment/Insurance" tab.

What are your rates?

Consultation (10-15 mins): Free. Individual Session (50-55 mins): $150. Couples Session (50-55 mins): $200. I can offer a sliding scale fee for students. Under the No Surprises Act, you have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate of what your services may cost.

Do you provide in person / in office services?

I am only seeing clients through teletherapy/online sessions. I am able to provide online counseling to anyone who is a Florida or Arizona resident.

What presenting problems do you treat?

Stress, Anxiety, Parenting Issues, Self esteem, ADHD, Depression, Relationship/Couple Issues, Interracial Relationships, Friendship Conflict, Post High School Stress, College/University Struggles, Family conflict, Career Difficulties, Coping With Life Changes, Coaching, Caregiver Issues and Stress, and Overall Emotional Distress.

How long does each therapy session last?

Each therapy session is about 50-55 minutes long.

What type of payment do you accept?

I accept credit card or debit card. I will send you a credit card form to fill out via email. I do require my clients to enroll in auto pay to prevent any late or missed payment. I will charge your session rate to your card automatically after your session.

What type of therapy technique do you use?

I use multiple treatment modalities to help you build your strengths and help you achieve all of your treatment plan goals at a pace that works for you. My goal as your therapist is to make you feel safe, affirmed and empowered: Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Solution Focused Therapy, Clinical Hypnosis, Eye Movement Desensitization Reprocessing (EMDR), Client-Centered Therapy, Mindfulness Therapy, Motivational Interviewing, Trauma-Focused Therapy.

Can I contact you by email?

Yes, feel free to contact me by email but please note that this is not a means of emergency contact. I will respond to you within 48 hours. Please do not email me any sensitive information in order to protect your privacy.

What age do you work with?

I work with clients aged 16 and up.

Is your practice inclusive?

Yes. I have experience working with people of color and the LGBTQIA community (as well as other marginalized groups). This is a no judgement, compassionate listening, and understanding practice.

Are there any areas you do not work with?

I do not conduct child custody evaluations or custody recommendations. I also do not generally provide treatment for eating disorders, psychotic disorders (schizophrenia, schizoaffective disorder, etc), or personality disorders. Due to state licensing laws, I can only provide therapy and counseling to individuals physically residing in Florida or Arizona.

What is your cancellation policy?

There is a 24 hour cancellation / reschedule policy. If you do not notify me at least 24 hours before your appointment, you will be charged a fee. Unless otherwise notified ahead of time, there will be a 10 minute grace period before the session is considered a no show. A no show appointment will be subjected to a full session charge. The fee will be charged to your card on file.

Do you speak any other language?

Yes. I speak fluently in Vietnamese.

How long will I be in therapy for?

That is entirely up to you based on your needs, availability, financials, etc. The majority of my clients I see on a weekly basis, while some are bi-weekly or as needed. In general, most clients are in therapy for at least a period of 3 months, if not much longer depending on their presenting problem. We will figure out what best suits your needs together.

What type of therapist are you?

I am a master's level, Licensed Clinical Social Worker.

Do you complete ESA letters?

No, I do not generally complete these letters. If you are coming to therapy for just this purpose, I recommend looking up ESA Doggy or Pettable.

In crisis? Call or text 988 anytime for free, confidential support. If there is an immediate emergency, call 911.
