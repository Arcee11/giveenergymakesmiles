14 Days of PeaceA guided journal to ease anxiety and gently restore calm. This journal is for the moments when your mind feels crowded and your heart feels tired.

Whether you are managing daily anxiety, emotional overwhelm or simply craving a sense of stillness, 14 Days of Peace offers a quiet space to slow down, reflect and reconnect with yourself. You do not need to be calm before you begin, this journal meets you exactly where you are.

With simple prompts, grounding tools and calming affirmations, each day is designed to support your nervous system and help you return to a place of peace, one page at a time. Each day includes: A mindful check in to connect with how you feel A gentle writing prompt to release mental noise A practical calming tool to use in daily life A soft affirmation to hold onto Space for personal reflection, rest or quiet celebration This journal is ideal for:• Individuals experiencing anxiety or high stress• Those seeking daily grounding and emotional regulation• Therapy or coaching clients needing structure between sessions• Anyone creating a softer, more spacious inner life There is no pressure here, No performance and No need to be perfect.

Just a safe place to breathe, to feel and to slowly let peace in again.
