14 Days of ConfidenceA guided journal to help you recognize your selfworth, take up space and build lasting confidence from within. Confidence is not something you wait for, it is something you build through presence, action and self trust.

This 14 day journal is your step by step guide to practice showing up boldly, speaking clearly and owning your voice. Each day includes: A confidence check in to reflect on your mindset and mood A focused journal prompt to help you take intentional action A practical tool to shift your energy or behavior A daily affirmation to reinforce your inner belief Space to celebrate your wins and track your progress You will explore real moments and strengthen the way you carry yourself.

Some days will stretch you while others will feel natural, all of it is part of the growth! Created by Give Energy Make Smiles, this journal is designed to help you stop hiding and start leading with truth.

Whether you are just beginning your confidence journey or looking for a reset, this two week experience offers structure, reflection and encouragement. This journal is ideal for:• Personal growth routines• Clients in therapy or coaching• Teens and adults working on self esteem• Anyone wanting to reclaim their presence in a gentle and grounded way Confidence is a practice.

Let this journal show you how far you have already come and how much power you already hold.
