14 Days of the In-BetweenA guided journal for healing the quiet space between siblings who were close, but never quite close enough. There is a quiet ache that lives in the space between childhoods.

Maybe you were the older sibling who helped raise the younger ones, or the one who was always a little too old or too young to belong fully. Maybe you were the bridge, the babysitter, the afterthought or the peacemaker.

You were part of the story, but never quite centered in it.This journal is for you. The sibling who was always there, but not always seen.

The one whose story unfolded just outside the spotlight. Over the course of 14 days, this guided journal offers prompts, tools, affirmations and reflection space to help you give language to your experience.

You will explore the impact of age gaps, unspoken expectations and quiet emotional labor. You will also practice releasing comparison and reclaiming the identity you formed in between the lines of everyone else’s chapters.

Each day includes: A mood and body check in for personal awareness A thoughtful prompt to explore a specific sibling dynamic A practical tool to process the past or connect the dots An affirmation to ground your experience Reflection space for personal insight and validation This journal is ideal for:• Adult siblings processing childhood roles• Individuals navigating estranged or distant sibling relationships• Therapy or support work related to family identity• Those who never fully felt like the oldest or youngest, just somewhere in the middle You were not too far apart to matter, you were growing into someone uniquely shaped by a rhythm no one else in your family experienced. This journal helps you honor that path and take up space as your full self.
