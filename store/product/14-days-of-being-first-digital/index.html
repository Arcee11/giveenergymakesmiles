14 Days of Being FirstA guided journal for healing the pressure and trauma of being the eldest child. Being the first often meant being the one who had to grow up faster.

You were the first to be called strong, the first to be asked to hold things together and the first to carry expectations that were never fully explained. This journal is for the one who had to be ready before they were.

14 Days of Being First is a space to set down the invisible weight. It is not about performance or being the example, it is about returning to yourself without the roles, the pressure or the fear of being too much or not enough.

It is about healing the patterns that shaped you when no one else was paying attention. Each day offers a gentle structure to reflect and reconnect with yourself: A check in to acknowledge your current mood and body A reflective prompt to explore early roles and hidden pressures A simple practice to process past patterns or meet current needs An affirmation to support your voice and presence Free space for reflection, emotional release or celebration This journal is ideal for:• Eldest children unpacking early responsibility• Adults healing from family expectations• Therapy clients or support groups exploring birth order roles• Anyone reclaiming a sense of self beyond being the strong one This is a quiet space to be seen as more than what you carried.

You were the first, but you do not have to carry it alone anymore. Let this journal be the beginning of coming home to yourself.
