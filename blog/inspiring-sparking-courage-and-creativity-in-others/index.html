Inspiring: Sparking Courage and Creativity in Others

January 28, 2026·By Kayla Luu, LCSW

What Inspiration Really Is

Inspiration is the ability to ignite hope, courage and new ideas in others through your words, actions or example. It's the spark that reminds people of what's possible and encourages them to reach beyond their comfort zones. Inspiring people live authentically, share their journeys honestly and empower others to believe in themselves.

Why Inspiration Matters

Inspiration creates momentum and possibility. It can transform discouragement into determination and hesitation into bold action. In communities, inspiration spreads like a ripple, motivating groups to work toward shared goals and brighter futures. For the Give Energy Make Smiles community, inspiration is central as it's how we encourage growth, kindness and resilience in ourselves and those around us.

The Science of Inspiring: Sparking Courage and Creativity in Others

Inspiration is more than a feeling, it is a spark that activates real change in the brain and body. Neuroscience shows that when people feel inspired, dopamine and serotonin increase, fueling motivation and emotional balance. At the same time, the brain's default mode network, which is linked to imagination and creative thinking, becomes more active. This explains why people often generate new ideas or feel the courage to take risks after hearing a story, seeing an act of bravery or witnessing creativity in action.

Research highlights that inspiration improves performance, deepens learning and strengthens connection. Students who feel inspired by a teacher perform better and engage more deeply in their studies. Athletes who are inspired by role models push through limits and discover new potential. In workplaces, teams that feel inspired by leaders report higher motivation, greater creativity and stronger collaboration. Inspiration does not just create energy in the moment, it creates momentum that leads to lasting progress.

You are practicing inspiration when your actions, words or example spark courage and creativity in others. For instance, a friend who shares their story of overcoming hardship may encourage someone else to keep going in the face of difficulty. A parent who pursues their goals openly and passionately may inspire their child to do the same. A leader who demonstrates creativity and curiosity can awaken fresh ideas in the people around them.

You know you are inspiring others when they begin to take action because of what they saw or heard from you. They may thank you for giving them courage, or you may notice them repeating habits or behaviors that you modeled. Inspiration shows itself in the shift from hesitation to action, from fear to courage and from doubt to creativity.

Opportunities to inspire are present every day. Sharing your passion for a project can motivate someone who feels stuck. Showing kindness during stress can remind others that compassion is possible even in hard times. Expressing hope when things look uncertain can help people believe in a brighter path forward. These moments may seem small, but they can ripple outward into families, workplaces and communities.

The science of inspiration proves that courage and creativity are contagious. By choosing to live in ways that spark energy and hope, you awaken the same qualities in others. Over time, this creates a cycle of growth where every inspired action fuels the next, building stronger individuals and stronger communities.

A Real Life Glimpse

A teacher shares their own struggles with learning to encourage a student who feels defeated. Seeing that someone they admire once faced similar hurdles sparks new determination in the student. The teacher's honesty and encouragement light a fire of belief, proof that inspiration often begins with simple authenticity.

Closing Thought

Inspiration is the spark that ignites courage and creativity. Every time you encourage, uplift or model perseverance, you remind others of their own potential. By choosing to inspire today, you create ripples of possibility that can reach far beyond what you see.
