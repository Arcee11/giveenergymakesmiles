Influential: Guiding and Uplifting Through Example

January 28, 2026·By Kayla Luu, LCSW

What Influence Really Is

Influence is the power to shape thoughts, actions or perspectives through your words, behavior and presence. True influence isn't about control, it's about guidance, integrity and inspiration. Influential people lead by example, demonstrating values and choices that uplift those around them.

Why Influence Matters

Influence creates ripples that extend far beyond the moment. It can spark change, inspire growth and strengthen communities. By acting with intention and authenticity, you build trust and open doors for meaningful progress. For the Give Energy Make Smiles community, influence is about using your presence to encourage, guide and empower others on their journeys.

The Science of Influential

Influence is not only about power or authority, it is about the subtle ways human behavior shapes those around us. Psychology shows that people learn more from observing than from being told what to do. This process, called social modeling, activates mirror neurons in the brain which allow us to reflect the actions, emotions and attitudes of others. When someone demonstrates patience, perseverance or kindness, the people around them are more likely to repeat those same qualities.

Research has found that leaders who lead through example create stronger teams, healthier work environments and longer lasting motivation. For instance, when a manager models open communication and respect, employees are more likely to mirror these behaviors in their own interactions. Parents who consistently show honesty and responsibility increase the likelihood that children will adopt these traits. Even in friendships, when one person practices optimism in difficult times, it can lift the emotional tone of the whole group.

You know you are using influence in a positive way when people respond to your actions without you needing to explain them. A friend may pick up journaling after noticing your daily habit. A coworker might show more patience with clients after watching how you stay calm under pressure. These shifts often happen quietly, but they show the science of influence at work.

Moments to use this quality appear every day. When you want to encourage a child to finish homework, showing your own focus and effort can be more powerful than repeated reminders. When you want to improve trust in your team, demonstrating transparency in your decisions can invite others to do the same. Even practicing small acts of generosity, like offering help or encouragement, can ripple outward into your community.

Influence grows strongest when it is consistent. Over time, people recognize patterns in your behavior and use those patterns as guidance for their own. By choosing to live the values you hope to see in others, you turn your daily actions into a steady message. Science confirms that example is one of the most powerful forms of teaching, and when used with care it uplifts, inspires and transforms the people around you.

A Real Life Glimpse

A community member quietly volunteers their time, supports others and treats everyone with kindness. Over time, their example inspires others to contribute and connect more deeply. Without fanfare, their influence reshapes the group's culture into one of generosity and care.

Closing Thought

Influence is guidance through example. Each choice you make has the power to inspire and encourage others. By using your influence with integrity and compassion today, you help create a culture of trust, growth and shared success.
